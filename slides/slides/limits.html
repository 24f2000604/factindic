What these results cannot tell you

LimitConsequence
No human evaluationAll precision rests on one automatic grader that reads machine translations
Small test set60 articles per language; moderate effects cannot be excluded
Claim recall near zeroDefined for 38% of items, so claim F1 is nearly uninformative
Rule-based RomanizationEasier than real user spelling; real input is likely to do worse
Mild code-mixingMixing index about 3 in the test bed; 4.1 (Hindi) and 6.5 (Gujarati) in natural articles
Weak native verifierStage 4 reranks on a noisy signal; a better verifier might do more
One seedThe second run differs in hardware and code, so seed variation is not measured
The most important one is the first. In H1, two automatic verifiers agreed only weakly, kappa 0.15, which is a warning against trusting any single one. Recall and F1 were restricted to items where recall is defined, and this was decided after seeing the results; it changes no conclusion.
