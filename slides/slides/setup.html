The test: same facts, three forms, an independent grader

360

Test items

60 Hindi and 60 Gujarati articles, each native, script-mixed and Romanized.

80

Natural articles

Really code-mixed ILSUM news, 40 per language. 440 items in all.

11

Main systems

Two fine-tuned baselines, Llama, five Gemma variants, decoding, drop-only repair, FactIndic.

1

Held-out grader

FactCG checks every summary sentence. It never trains or reranks.

Every system is graded against the native article, whatever form it read. Differences are paired by item, with 95% bootstrap intervals.

Sizes are smaller than the plan, which asked for 250 articles per language. A separate comparison of five decoders uses 240 items, and Chain-of-Verification uses 60. The natural code-mixed articles average a mixing index of 4.1 for Hindi and 6.5 for Gujarati; the plan asked for 8, which too few clean articles reach.
