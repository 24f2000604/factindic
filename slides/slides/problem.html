Fluent summaries still state things the article never said

Readers write Hindi and Gujarati in several forms. The fact checkers are built for English.

Native Hindi

भारत में प्रदूषण का स्तर लगातार बढ़ रहा है।

Script-mixed

भारत में pollution का level लगातार increase हो रहा है।

Romanized

Bharat mein pradushan ka star lagatar badh raha hai.

Script-mixed Gujarati

ભારતમાં pollution નું level સતત increase થઈ રહ્યું છે.

Illustrative sentences, not test items

Same fact, different surface. A summarizer has to read all of these and answer in the native script of the matrix language. The question of the project: does making a small open model factuality-aware actually reduce unsupported statements, and does that depend on the form of the input?
